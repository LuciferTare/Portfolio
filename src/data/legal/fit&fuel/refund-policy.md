This Refund Policy covers payments for Fit&Fuel plans (Starter, Growth, and Scale). It is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/fit&fuel/terms-conditions/).

## 7-day refund

If Fit&Fuel isn't right for your gym, you can ask for a **full refund within 7 days** of paying for a plan. This applies to new plans and to renewals, for every billing period: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and annual.

The 7 days start on the date of payment.

## After 7 days

After 7 days, payments are not refundable, including for the unused part of a billing period. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again unless you renew.

## Payment errors

Whatever the date, we refund the payment in full if you were charged twice for the same plan and period, or charged for a renewal you had cancelled before its renewal date.

## How to ask for a refund

Email [fitandfuel26@gmail.com](mailto:fitandfuel26@gmail.com) with:

- the email address of the gym owner's account;
- the gym's name;
- the plan, the amount, and the date of payment;
- the payment reference or transaction ID, if you have it.

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank or payment provider may take a few more days to show it.

When a refund is approved, the gym's plan ends on the date of the refund.

## Purchases through Google Play

If you bought a plan through Google Play, Google's refund policies also apply. You can ask Google for a refund through Google Play, or email us and we will help.

## Payments to your gym

Payments that members make to their gym, recorded in Fit&Fuel as membership payments, are between the member and the gym. For a refund of a gym membership fee, contact your gym.
