This page explains how to delete your account in **Fit&Fuel**, the gym management app developed by **Sushant Avinash Tare**, and what happens to your data.

## How to ask for deletion

Email [fitandfuel26@gmail.com](mailto:fitandfuel26@gmail.com) from the email address you use to sign in to Fit&Fuel, with the subject **"Delete my Fit&Fuel account"**. Include:

- your name and the email address of your account;
- your role (gym owner, trainer, or member) and your gym's name;
- whether you want your whole account deleted, or only some of your data (for example, attendance photos).

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from that email address before we delete anything. We confirm by email once deletion is complete.

If you are a trainer or member, you can also ask your gym owner to remove you from the gym in the app.

## What gets deleted

Within **30 days** of confirming your request, we delete:

- your account and sign-in details (email address and password);
- your profile: phone number, name, date of birth, gender, profile picture, role, and status;
- your body measurements;
- your attendance check-in and check-out photos and locations;
- your device's notification token.

**If you are a gym owner,** deleting your account also deletes your gym and all of its data: its trainers, members, memberships, payments, and attendance records. Export or note anything you need before you ask.

Copies in our backups are removed as the backups are replaced, within a further 90 days.

## What we may keep

- **Payment and membership records** that a gym or we must keep for tax, accounting, or legal reasons. These are kept only as long as the law requires, and are not used for anything else.
- **Crash reports** in Firebase Crashlytics, which contain a user ID but not your name or contact details. Google deletes them automatically under its retention schedule, which is currently 90 days.
- **Data on your phone:** data saved on your device for offline use stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
