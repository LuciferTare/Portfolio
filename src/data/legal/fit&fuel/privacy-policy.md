This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Fit&Fuel Android app ("Fit&Fuel", "the app") collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses the app: gym owners, trainers, and members.

## Who we are

Fit&Fuel is developed and operated by **Sushant Avinash Tare**, an individual developer based in Palghar, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [fitandfuel26@gmail.com](mailto:fitandfuel26@gmail.com).

## How Fit&Fuel is used

Fit&Fuel is a gym management app. A **gym owner** creates a gym and adds **trainers** and **members** to it. Each person signs in with their own account.

The gym decides whose details go into the app and why. For the personal data of its trainers and members, the gym is responsible for having a lawful reason to collect it and for telling those people. We store and process that data on the gym's behalf, to run the app for that gym.

## Data we collect

| Category                 | What it includes                                                                                                                                           | When it is collected                                      |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account                  | Email address and password (for sign-in), phone number, name, date of birth, gender, profile picture, role (owner, trainer, or member), and account status | When an account is created or its profile is updated      |
| Location                 | Precise GPS location                                                                                                                                       | Only at the moment of an attendance check-in or check-out |
| Photos                   | A photo taken with the camera                                                                                                                              | At each attendance check-in and check-out                 |
| Body measurements        | Weight, height, body fat, and body circumferences                                                                                                          | When they are entered in the app                          |
| Memberships and payments | Membership plan, start and end dates, amount paid, payment mode (cash or online), and payment records                                                      | When the gym records a membership or a payment            |
| Crash reports            | User ID, role, device and app details, and technical details of the crash                                                                                  | Automatically, when the app crashes                       |
| Notification token       | A device token for push notifications                                                                                                                      | When notifications are set up on the device               |

We do **not** collect location in the background. The app reads your location only when you check in or out.

We do **not** process card or bank details. Payments that members make to their gym are recorded in the app as entries (amount, date, and mode); the money itself does not pass through Fit&Fuel.

## How we use data

- To create and secure your account, and to verify your email address.
- To run the features you use: trainer and member management, memberships, payments, attendance, and reports.
- To confirm attendance with the location and photo taken at check-in and check-out.
- To send notifications about your gym, account, or membership.
- To find and fix crashes and keep the app reliable.
- To answer your support requests.
- To meet legal obligations and enforce our [Terms and Conditions](/fit&fuel/terms-conditions/).

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

## Who can see your data

- **Within your gym:** the gym owner sees the gym's trainers, members, memberships, payments, and attendance. A trainer sees only the active members assigned to them. Members see their own details.
- **Other gyms:** cannot see your gym's data.
- **Service providers:** we use Google Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. Our servers store the app's data so it can be backed up and synced between devices.
- **Legal reasons:** we may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or us.

## Storage and security

The app saves data on the phone so it works without internet, and backs it up to our server when the phone is back online. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by role, as described above.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## How long we keep data

We keep account and gym data for as long as the account or gym is active. Attendance photos and locations are kept as part of the gym's attendance history for as long as the gym keeps that history.

When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise its personal data as described on the [Delete your account](/fit&fuel/delete-account/) page. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it, for example payment records needed for tax or accounting.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
- ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data;
- ask us to delete your data, or withdraw your consent;
- nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
- raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.

You can update most profile details in the app. For anything else, email [fitandfuel26@gmail.com](mailto:fitandfuel26@gmail.com). If you are a trainer or member, you can also ask your gym, which manages your details in the app.

## Device permissions

| Permission                       | Why the app asks for it                                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Camera                           | To take the attendance photo at check-in and check-out, and a profile picture |
| Precise and approximate location | To confirm you are at the gym when you check in or out                        |
| Notifications                    | To show reminders and updates about your gym and membership                   |
| Internet                         | To sign in, back up, and sync data                                            |
| Wake lock and foreground service | To keep media playback and syncing running reliably while the app is in use   |
| Media playback                   | To play workout audio and video                                               |

You can turn permissions off in your phone's settings. Some features, such as check-in, won't work without them.

## Children

Fit&Fuel is meant to be used by gyms and the adults who train there. If a gym adds a member who is under 18, the gym must first get verifiable consent from the member's parent or lawful guardian. If you believe a child's data was added without that consent, email us and we will delete it.
